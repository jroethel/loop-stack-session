## Outcome

Every work session in a conforming repo leaves a durable record of what ran, where it stopped, and why,
written incrementally so a quota-killed session still leaves one.
Handoffs stop accumulating: consumption is recorded per action in an append-only transition log and archival fires automatically.
The three empty board lanes (`blocked-on-fact`, `handed-off`, the session half of `done`) populate from these records,
and went-stale and died-mid-work states become visible without anyone recording anything at the moment of death.
Standalone value holds even with no board rendered: a session asked to resume reads recorded state
and names the true frontier (the original #52 ask).

## End artifact

and the convention propagated to the other conforming repos on this Mac via the template-version mechanism.
Proof of value: sys-prompts-cc's recorded state names its authoritative handoff, with the older one consumed and archived
(parent criterion 10, parked for exactly this slice).

## Done looks like

Carried from the parent's Done-looks-like and seam-1 paragraph, bounded to this slice:

- Session cards live in `docs/sessions/`: one card per session, created at session start, patched in place as work proceeds,
  closed with a terminal status from a closed vocabulary.
  A card with a start and no close is, by construction, a session that died mid-work.
- Session cards are harness-agnostic: no field assumes Claude Code, so any harness (pi, opencode, just) can write and read them.
- The recording tax is capped at that one card; anything derivable comes from take-stock derivation, never hand-recording.
- Handoff consumption requires a per-action disposition (`executed|superseded`); dispositions append to a transition log
  (action, disposition, when), never mutate a status field, and "current state" is always derived from the log.
- With all dispositions present, archival fires automatically and is verbose-announced under archive rule 5;
  `docs/handoffs/` holds only live handoffs.
- A new `lifecycle-lint` class h enforces the handoff lifecycle for handoffs created on or after the adoption date only
  (`lifecycle-lint-since:` gate mirroring `filename-grammar-since:`); the 20 pre-adoption handoffs here are grandfathered,
  with a one-time bulk backfill offered under human confirmation, never forced through the done-gate.
- A take-stock script rebuilds a repo's recorded state on demand from git, handoffs, and the tracker.
- The board reads the new source: `board-cards.sh` stops skipping `blocked-on-fact`, `handed-off`, and `done`
  (`scripts/board-cards.sh:104`), session-sourced cards land in their lanes, a went-stale repo and a died-mid-work session
  render their markers, and the `done` lane joins the tracker half through a bounded closed-issue lookback
  (the named post-MVP pivot from the handoff).
- `template-version` rolls 6 to 7 and propagates via `/loop-setup` reconcile to the conforming repos on this Mac,
  carrying the `rubix-autorun` drift fix and the `docs/spikes/` import-sweep exclusion with it.

## Approach

Chosen in the parent: judgment facts are recorded once where they happen, everything derivable is computed fresh.
The convention is doctrine plus enforcement, not a daemon: conventions prose says what a session writes,
templates give the shape, lint class h and the done-gate catch the misses, take-stock backfills what recording skipped.
Rollout is piloted per the parent: this repo and sys-prompts-cc first, proof-of-value validated, then the template roll.
This run dogfoods itself: the seam-1 session writes the first real session card.

## Success criteria

1. `[executed-check]` Marking a handoff consumed without per-action dispositions is rejected; dispositions append to the
   transition log rather than mutating a status field; with dispositions, archival fires automatically and is announced;
   lint class h exits non-zero on a consumed-unarchived handoff and ignores handoffs dated before adoption. (Parent 6.)
2. `[executed-check]` Standalone resume: in a pilot repo with no board rendered, the recorded state names the authoritative
   handoff and the next step. (Parent 7.)
3. `[executed-check]` A repo with commits newer than its recorded state renders the went-stale marker;
   a session card opened but never closed renders a died-mid-work marker. (Parent 9.)
4. `[executed-check]` Proof of value: sys-prompts-cc's recorded state names the authoritative handoff and the older one is
   consumed and archived. (Parent 10.)
5. `[executed-check]` Board lanes populate: a session card closed `blocked` renders in `blocked-on-fact`, a live handoff
   renders in `handed-off`, and `done` joins closed session cards with tracker issues from the bounded lookback;
   re-render after each state change moves the card.
6. `[executed-check]` Propagation: `/loop-setup` reconcile rolls template-version 7 to the conforming repos on this Mac
   and `lifecycle-lint.sh` passes in each afterward.
7. `[judgment]` After one real work session under the convention, the recording tax feels acceptable and the card answers
   "where did I leave off" without reconstruction. (Criteria 1-6 cover mechanics; the tax call stays human.)
